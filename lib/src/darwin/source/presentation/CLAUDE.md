# The Presentation layer

**Position.** Fourth of the four ([ADR 0001](../../../../documentation/gui/adrs/layer-enforcement.md)): the windows, views and rendering, in SwiftUI -- whose types stay here and nowhere below.

**Modules:** `styles` (the shared visual vocabulary, and the theme switch: the design's three themes or the system's, and the text size; and the common components, among them the markdown renderer every chat answer draws through, and the runtime install's consent sheet and card that Video, Compose and Voice share), `shell` (the window around every screen: the sidebar and its places, the route, the keys, the overlays and the toast, and the window law; and the parity table, every CLI command's place in the app, ADR 0003), `session` (one chat session: the window, its transcript and cards, over a SwiftUI-free view-model folding the session's events, paused and resumed by the library, the suite it runs under chosen -- moved by a relaunch, said, since machine mode carries no suite -- and an execute session's banner, its plays and their stages narrated), `sessions` (the Sessions place: the saved conversations' list and its changes, the one live session -- opened or moved under a suite, the CLI's verdict on each read for the chooser -- the inspector, and the execute door), `models` (the Models place: the library of every configured and stored model under the CLI's groups, a model's details from its models info document -- the typed facts where the CLI states them, and a backend's adapters with the doctor's word on their pins, edited as one list -- each MLX set's kind said beside its format and only that kind's actions offered, a drafter paired with a base the binary offers it and a backend's drafter unpaired, the convert sheet running the converter the CLI names, delete, rename and repair asked and read back, and the roles moved through their verbs), `tasks` (the Tasks place: the ledger's tasks read on focus, a run started from the new-task sheet -- a goal written, or a recipe with its expansion shown -- and followed live from its events, halt, cancel and resume as their verbs), `monitor` (the system monitor overlay: the system read every two seconds while open, and the app's own children's memory), `suites` (the Suites place: each suite's members read through config get, its fit the CLI's own verdict from models status where it states one -- else an estimate worked out here, said so -- and a chat opened under it or the open session moved to it), `graph` (the Code graph place: a graph's query drawn by a seeded, deterministic layout computed in its view-model, a node explained, its neighbours walked, a rebuild supervised), `video` (the Video place's clips: the records in ~/Movies/Apogee and added folders, a clip's page and the engine its record ran on, the generate sheet's command as typed with the engine its model runs on, the run supervised, the install asked first), `compose` (compositions: a brief composed with its scenes read from the project document as the composer saves it, its voice model named where the store holds more than one and its captions' fate said before the run, stopped and resumed by the same command, a plan said as planned, edited by instruction with its diff and its preview supervised, the install asked first, the projects passed over listed), `voice` (the Voice place: the speeches in ~/Music/Apogee and added folders, say with the voice model picked where the store holds more than one, its engine and its own voices, playback lit by the record's sentence timings), `settings` (the Settings window: General, Appearance, Backends & keys, Video & voice, Permissions -- each over its verbs with read-back, a key sent on stdin and kept nowhere), `console` (the console: any apogee command run as typed on the jobs core, completion asked of the binary, the parity table's refusals -- the carve-outs, the doors and the two edit verbs, by the command path past the root options -- refused with their reasons), `knowledge` (the Knowledge place: the decision records -- list by branch, ask with scores, capture supervised, link, move, delete, export -- and the collections -- health, sources, search as the CLI prints it, ingest supervised, deletes confirmed), `training` (the Training place: runs launched, gated, promoted -- merged, or the adapter alone -- and rolled back, the regimes, the datasets pulled and their kits -- every long act supervised, nothing started but by the person, a refused promotion's sheet opened again with the CLI's words), `library` (the Library place: agents, symphonies and MCP servers over their verbs -- a play supervised, the person's own definition files edited in place and checked by the binary where it can), `review` (the Review place: a merge request reviewed dry run first, its findings drawn and posted only on the person's click on the batch; the sweep's targets and a sweep supervised; the review skills read), `app` (the application: its scenes and the composition of its view-models, each place's screen mounted in the shell).

**Shape** ([ADR 0002](../../../../documentation/gui/adrs/presentation-mvvm.md)): every module but `styles` is `Views/` + `ViewModels/`, with any further directory it genuinely needs; a view binds to its view-model, the view-model consumes Business, and no file under `ViewModels/` imports a UI framework. `styles` -- colors, type, spacing and the common components -- leans on no sibling, and every other module may use it; a component a second module wants is promoted there, never copied.

**Seen on its own** ([ADR 0004](../../../../documentation/gui/adrs/module-schemes-and-previews.md)): every module, `styles` and `app` included, has its own shared scheme named for its target, which builds it and runs its tests alone; every view it draws has a named `#Preview` in its own file, built over the module's own fakes and the binary's recorded documents in `Previews/`. All of it sits inside `#if DEBUG`, and a preview never starts the binary.

**The entry point** is not a module: the application target `Apogee` builds `source/main/`, one file that starts `PresentationApp`'s `ApogeeApp` -- the composition root, as the CLI's `source/main.cpp` is -- and embeds exactly the frameworks its links reach at run time, so a module a Presentation module starts linking is embedded the same day.

**May import:** every layer below, the UI frameworks, and `styles` from any module.

**Enforced by:** [`tests/architecture/layering`](../../tests/architecture/layering/LayeringCheck.swift): the edges, the MVVM shape, the view-models' imports, `styles`' independence, each module's scheme and every view's preview.

**Changing this layer:** a new module is a directory `source/presentation/<package>/` with `Views/` and `ViewModels/`, a framework target named `Presentation<Package>` in `Apogee.xcodeproj` building that folder, its test bundle at `tests/presentation/<package>/` in the shared scheme, its own shared scheme `Apogee.xcodeproj/xcshareddata/xcschemes/Presentation<Package>.xcscheme` building the target and running that bundle, a `#Preview` for every view it draws with its fakes and documents in `Previews/`, and its name on this card's **Modules** line.
