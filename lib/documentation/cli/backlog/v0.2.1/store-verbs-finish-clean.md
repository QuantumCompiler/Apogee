# Model-store verbs finish clean: a pull stopped mid-quantize cleans up, `train promote --as` keeps adapter pins honest, `models delete` reads the video default per set, a rename carries conversion records, the mlx-video pin remedy works

**What / why.** The model store's verbs each promise to leave the store and config as they say they did: a stopped pull removes what it staged and exits 130 (55c), a base moved under pinned adapters is refused or re-pinned (55e), a delete warns only about the pointers it actually strands (55d), a rename carries every pointer into the moved folder (M25, 46a), and `check`'s remedy for an off-pin runtime fixes it (M23). The second CLI/GUI bug sweep (2026-10-31) found five places where a verb reports success or a cleanup it did not do. Each finding was re-read at HEAD the same day. Paths are under `lib/src/cli/source/`.

1. **A pull stopped during the register chain's quantize stage dies of the signal, with no `error` and its staging left behind** (incomplete fix of 55c, MILESTONES.md:10735 "Every `models pull` path is cancellable through one `InterruptScope`"; it also contradicts the chain milestone's "Ctrl-C cleaning up" for every stage, MILESTONES.md:3655). `quantize_model` (`presentation/cli/models_pull.cpp:2682`) runs the in-process quantizer at `:2719-2724` under a `BusyLine` with no `InterruptScope`. The only scope on that path is inside `commit_with_progress` (`:217`), which runs after the quantizer returns. The chain's quantize stage lambda (`:3094-3103`) has no scope of its own, and neither does the register stage (`:3106-3116`) or `run_chain` (`presentation/cli/model_chain.cpp:30-90`). Only the download stage has one (`models_pull.cpp:3057`). The standalone `models quantize` verb reaches the same function at `:4134` with no scope either. `InterruptScope` installs its handler in its constructor and restores the previous one in its destructor (`presentation/cli/interrupt.cpp:24-32`), and the only other `signal(SIG…` calls in the tree are `presentation/httpserver/serve.cpp:189-196` and `infrastructure/platform/child_process.cpp:100` (SIGPIPE). So a SIGINT during quantization takes the default action. Two facts make "act on it when the quantizer returns" no fix: `llama_model_quantize_params` carries no abort hook (the vendored `llama.h`), and the GUI's Stop sends SIGINT, then SIGTERM after a 10-second grace and SIGKILL 2 seconds later (`lib/src/darwin/source/business/jobs/SupervisedJob.swift:104-112`), and nothing outside `serve` handles SIGTERM. The header claims the opposite: `models_pull.h:622-627` says "for the chain, over its download, each later stage under its own -- so what it staged is removed, the exit is 130, and a stream ends on its `error`". `reference/machine-mode.md:410-416` names only "the register chain's download". Scenario: `apogee models pull org/model --safetensors --register-with Q4_K_M --output-format stream-json`, then the GUI's Stop (or Ctrl-C) during the quantize stage. The process dies of SIGINT, not a clean exit 130. The stream's last line is a `stage` `started` or a notice, with no `error`. The chain never says `stopped at … resume with` (`model_chain.cpp:50-62` runs only on a thrown failure). Its "from here on, if this stops, resume with" line (`:69-73`) is said only when a stage changed the resume command, so a pull chain said it once after its download and a convert-only chain never did. A partial `<store>/<model>/gguf/.incoming-*/…-Q4_K_M.gguf` stays on disk, invisible to `models list`, until `apogee check` reports it as abandoned staging. Source only, not probed: no quantizable GGUF fixture is available without a model. The Ctrl-C lost in the gap after `pull_snapshot` returns is a small edge case of the same gap, not the main bug.

2. **A full-weight `train promote <run> --as <existing entry>` moves `model_path` under hand-pinned adapters without the 55e check** (incomplete fix of 55e, MILESTONES.md:10854: "`set-backend --model-path` moved the base under pinned adapters silently"; 55e's title is "every path honors the adapter set and the pin"). When the promote updates an existing entry, `train.cpp:821-826` calls `harness::set_backend_model_path(content, ledger.backend, outcome.weights_path)` through the one editor. In `precheck_promotion` (`presentation/cli/train.cpp:455`), every adapter reference sits under `if (request.adapter)` (`:461`, `:537`). The 55e check is `repin_kept_adapters` (`presentation/operations/backend_fields.cpp:88`), and only `set_backend_fields` calls it (`:441`). Adapter versions the ledger records from an earlier `train promote --adapter` are already covered: `target_conflict` (`business/training/promote.cpp:264-285`) refuses a merged promote into a ledger that holds them, with "a merged version would replace that base under them" (`:281-283`). The gap is adapters added to the entry by hand with `config set-backend --adapters`. Scenario: `apogee config set-backend foo --adapters ~/lora.gguf` (pinned to foo's base), then `apogee train promote <run> --as foo`. The promote succeeds and says to chat with foo. `apogee chat -m foo` is then refused by `adapter_refusal` (`data/backends/adapters.cpp:326-334`, "the adapter … was pinned to other base weights"). Nothing warned at promote time, and neither of 55e's two ways out (re-pin, or `--adapters ""`) was named. The recorded decision at MILESTONES.md:10875 ("Rollback checks only the version's own adapter, not adapters added by hand") covers `train rollback` only, not promote. Source only, not probed: a train run is needed.

3. **`models delete` treats a `models.default_video` that names a model as stranded when only one of that model's video sets goes, and `--and-backends` clears it** (55d's reference walk over-matches, MILESTONES.md:10798: "The default pipeline: deleting it warns, and clears `models.default_video`"). `points_inside` (`presentation/operations/store_references.cpp:200-212`) ends with `return within(target.path, dir) || (within(dir, target.path) && runs_video(dir));` (`:211`). For a pointer that names a bare model, it matches any video set under that model (`runs_video`, `:92-99`: a pipeline, or an MLX directory of kind `video`). `stranded_by` (`presentation/cli/models_pull.cpp:292`, called at `:4018` with the whole plan's `removes`) then prints the "finds nothing until it names another" warning, and `clear_references` (`store_references.cpp:296`) empties the pointer under `--and-backends`. The rule `video generate` reads a bare model by is "the one video model it holds": `resolve_video_pipeline` (`presentation/cli/video_generate.cpp:437`) refuses a model holding two with `'<model>' holds 2 video models -- name one` (`:523-530`). So before the delete, the pointer already fails to resolve. Deleting one of the two sets fixes the pointer instead of stranding it, and the delete throws it away anyway. Scenario: `models.default_video: org--clip`, and org--clip stores two pipeline sets (for example, a second revision pulled after the pointer was set). `apogee models delete org--clip/pipeline/bbbbbbbbbbbb --yes --and-backends` prints the stranded warning and empties `models.default_video`. After the delete, `org--clip` would have resolved to the remaining set. Probe (scratchpad p3, sandbox `APOGEE_HOME`; two fake pipelines under org--clip, `default_video` hand-set to `"org--clip"`): the delete printed "models.default_video names this, and 'apogee video generate' without --model finds nothing until it names another. --and-backends: models.default_video is cleared too". The config then held `"default_video": ""`, with `aaaaaaaaaaaa` still stored. Reach is narrow: `config set-default-video org--clip` refuses while the model holds two sets, so this state needs a hand edit or a second pull after the pointer was set.

4. **`check`'s off-pin remedy for mlx-video does nothing: pip skips a VCS reinstall when the version is unchanged** (regression of M23, `mlx-runtimes-in-check`: "Below a floor or off a pin is a warning naming `apogee train setup --with <set>`"). `check.cpp:2053-2061` returns `OffPin` with `setup_remedy(installed.set, on)`, which is `apogee train setup --with mlx-video`. `PythonEnv::install` (`business/training/python_env.cpp:867`) builds the pip argv at `:887-891`, `-m pip install --disable-pip-version-check` plus `packages_for(set)` and no reinstall flag. For mlx-video those packages are `mlx-video @ git+https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-video.git@87db56a51758…` and `torch>=2.6` (`:164-167`). `train setup` prints `installed` whenever pip exits 0 (`presentation/cli/train.cpp:2072`). `read_installed_set` marks a pinned set `Current` only when `direct_url.json`'s `commit_id` equals the pin (`python_env.cpp:722-728`). This breaks only pinned (git-commit) sets, which today means mlx-video alone (`pinned_source`, `:392-397`), and only when the installed commit and the pinned commit declare the same version. Floor-based sets are fine, because pip upgrades a version below its floor. Scenario: mlx-video was installed the README way (`pip install git+https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-video.git`, main HEAD), or a later Apogee bumps the pin, and both commits declare the same version (0.1.0 in M23's own example). `apogee check` says it is off its pin and to run `apogee train setup --with mlx-video`. Running that, pip resolves the pinned commit, sees mlx-video 0.1.0 installed, and installs nothing. Apogee prints `installed`, `direct_url.json` still names the old commit, and the next `check` shows the same warning. Probe 1 (sandbox venv holding `mlx_video-0.1.0.dist-info` whose `direct_url.json` names commit `1111…`): check printed `warn video mlx-video 0.1.0 at commit 111111111111 … off its pin, commit 87db56a51758 (2026-05-13) -- the one commit this Apogee trusts / run: apogee train setup --with mlx-video`. Probe 2 (scratch venv, pip 26.1.1, a local git package `fakepin` 0.1.0 at commits C1 then C2): after installing at C1, `pip install "fakepin @ git+file://…@C2"` printed `Resolved … to commit 26c7ded5…` and nothing more, with no `Successfully installed`. `direct_url.json` still named C1 (`523434cf…`), and `import fakepin; fakepin.X` was still 1 (C1's value).

5. **A rename leaves every conversion record's `ref` naming the old folder, so `video generate` on an MLX Wan conversion loses its tokenizer and tells the user to re-pull** (the M25 class, `drafters-follow-renames`, and 46a, `rename-propagation`: a rename keeps the pointers into a moved folder; 53l's tokenizer pointer is left stale). None of the three rename paths rewrites it. `rename_backend_everywhere` (`presentation/operations/renames.cpp:213`), `rename_model_family` (`:322`) and `rename_stored_model` (`:504`, after moving the directories at `:623-632`) each call only `rename_drafter_bases` (`:123-143`, called at `:310`, `:450` and `:662`), and through it `models::rename_snapshot_base` (`data/modelstore/snapshot.cpp:114-139`), which touches `bases` alone.    - (b) `mlx_video_conversions` (`data/modelstore/store.cpp:1121-1146`) matches on `record->ref`, so the source's "made from it" goes empty.
   - (c) Lineage for every converted set (GGUF, MLX, voice) reads "source snapshot no longer on disk" (`presentation/cli/models.cpp:139`, `:246`).

   Scenario: store `wan--x` holds `safetensors/aaaa` (with `google/umt5-xxl/`) and its MLX conversion `mlx/bbbb`, whose record has ref `wan--x/safetensors/aaaa`. `video generate -m wan--x/mlx/bbbb` gets past the tokenizer rung. What already works: every other `models pull` path, and the chain's download, stops clean on Ctrl-C (`models_pull_cancel_test.cpp`); a merged promote into a ledger holding adapter versions is refused (`target_conflict`); `set-backend --model-path` re-pins or refuses kept adapters (55e); a delete of a model's only video set warns and clears correctly; floor-based Python sets upgrade on `train setup`; a drafter's `bases` follow a rename (M25).

**Core constraint(s).**
- **One config mutation path** (⚠ invariant): the promote's refusal comes before its `edit_config_file`, and `--and-backends`' clearing stays `clear_references` through the one editor. No new writer appears.
- **A child's stderr is captured, never inherited** (⚠ invariant): if the quantizer moves into a child of Apogee's own (open call), that child gets the mandatory sink and its bounded tail, and llama.cpp's log stays off the terminal as M3 has it.
- **One message for every surface** (ADR 0002): a stopped pull ends the same way on the terminal (exit 130, `cancelled -- nothing was written`) and in machine mode (the stream's last line is its `error`). The GUI shows what the stream says and needs no change.
- **A stopped job leaves nothing staged** (55c): every stage of a chain, and the standalone `models quantize`, removes its `.incoming-*` on a stop.
- **An adapter applies only over the weights it was pinned to** (53b, 55e): no path moves a base under a pinned adapter without either re-pinning it or refusing.
- **A record is rewritten only where it re-serializes byte for byte** (M25's rule for `rename_snapshot_base`): `ref` follows a rename under the same guard, and a record that would not round-trip is left alone and said.
- **A remedy `check` prints, followed, fixes what it named**, and `train setup` says `installed` only when the set reads `Current` afterwards.
- **Development runs on the dev channel**: live passes use `apogee-dev` over a temporary `APOGEE_HOME` in the scratchpad, never `apogee` or `~/.apogee`.

**Decisions made** (dated):
- 2026-10-31: Specced from the second CLI/GUI bug sweep (the user's call: every finding becomes a v0.2.1 item in its queue, CLI track 57, GUI track 6).
- 2026-10-31: One item: the five findings are the model store's verbs (`models pull`/`quantize`, `train promote`, `models delete`, `train setup`'s pinned reinstall, the three renames). Each reports a cleanup, a check or a fix it did not do, and they share the store's record and reference code.
- 2026-10-31: Finding 1 is not fixed by stopping when the quantizer returns. llama.cpp's quantizer takes no abort hook, and the GUI's Stop escalates to SIGTERM after 10 seconds, so the quantize has to be stoppable while it runs.
- 2026-10-31: Finding 2's door is a refusal, not a re-pin. An adapter trained over the old base never fits a fine-tune merged over it, the same reason `target_conflict` refuses a merged promote over ledger-held adapters. So 55e's re-pin door does not apply here, and only taking the adapters off or promoting under a new name do.

**Open calls:**
- [default: the quantizer runs as a child of Apogee's own (a hidden `__quantize` verb, its per-tensor progress over a pipe, its stderr a bounded tail), so a stop terminates it at once through the child-process path conversions already use. - [default: the chain runs under one scope across `run_chain`'s loop, so the gaps between stages are covered too. Whether the stages' own nested scopes stay or fold into it is settled at build against `interrupt.cpp`'s one global token.]
