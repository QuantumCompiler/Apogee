Window layout

Three columns under one toolbar. Drawn at half size.

sidebar · 256 system strip
toolbar · 52 · title, model, thinking, context
conversation · column max 860 gutter 76 · who and when message box
inspector · 304
  • Sidebar 256, resizable 220–360. Hides with ⌃⌘S.
  • Inspector 304. Hides with ⌥⌘I. Below a 1100 px window it floats over the conversation instead of pushing it.
  • Conversation column caps at 860 so lines stay readable on wide screens; the extra width becomes margin.
  • Smallest window 760 × 520. Below 760 the sidebar collapses to a button.
