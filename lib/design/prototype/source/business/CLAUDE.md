# The Business layer

**Position.** Third of the four ([the design's ADR 0001](../../../../documentation/adrs/design/react-design-prototype.md), applying [GUI ADR 0001](../../../../documentation/adrs/gui/layer-enforcement.md)): the services the view-models consume, behind the Mac app's own interfaces, so a later port swaps a real CLI in under the same names.

**Modules:** `reads` (CommandRunning, its five read outcomes and four command outcomes, and ReadRunner over the process stand-in), `jobs` (a job as a stream of updates, ended last, replayed from a recording on the clock; every child the app runs in one list, and a job's progress folded from its updates, 1h), `session` (a chat from ChatStarting, replaying a recorded machine-mode stream, a turn a send, waiting at each question). All three are design 1d's; media follows with 1k, and the ADR lists all four.

**What they hold**, each under the Mac app's name: `CommandRunning`, `ReadOutcome`, `CommandOutcome`, `ReadWithStderr`, `ReadRunner` and `SystemReading` (the `system` document, 1e), and the reads a session's header and chooser take (1u): `Field`, `ModelKind`, `ModelRow` and `ModelInfo` (`models list` and `models info`), `SuiteFootprint` and `Suites` (`models status --suite` and `config get suites`), and `Symphony` (`symphonies list`), a resumed chat's turns (1v): `SavedChat` (`chats show`); and the saved chats (1g): `ChatSummary`, `ChatDetails` and `RecallListing` (`chats list`, `chats info`, `chats recall`); the store (1h): `ModelRow`'s every field, `MLXKind`, `ModelInfo.read`, `RoleAssignment`, `BackendAdapter` and `AdapterCheck`; Settings' (1i): `CheckReport` and `CheckRow` (`check`), `Permissions`, `AllowedHosts` and `ServerTools` (the config verbs' reads), `Provider`, `ProviderRegistration` and `KeyedProviders` (`providers scan`, `providers register`, `auth list`), `MCPPreset` (`mcp presets`), and `AgentRegistration`, `XcodeCheck` and `MCPServerEntry` (Xcode's); Training's (1j): `TrainingRun`, `VersionLedger`, `TrainingStatus`, `PipelineRunRow`, `PipelineRun`, `PipelineSpec`, `CycleHeadline`, `CycleHistory`, `TrainingRegime`, `TrainingKit` and `TrainingDataset` (`train status`, `train versions`, `train pipeline list | status`, `train cycle status`, `train regime list`, `datasets kits | list`); `JobStarting`, `JobRunning`, `JobUpdate`, `JobEnd`, `JobStarter` and `SupervisedJob` (its end held back by its rule's `exitAfter`, 1v; listing each job in the registry it is given, 1h), `ChildRegistry` and `JobProgress` (1h; a pipeline's stages with their runs and scores, the trainer's iteration and a cycle pass's narration, 1j), a job's stderr played as its busy line where its rule says `busy` (1j); `ChatStarting`, `ChatSession`, `SessionEvent`, `SessionEnd`, `ChatLaunch`, `ApogeeClient` and `MachineSession` (a freeze at a named event, an exit replayed after the input closes, a restart, a continuation picked by what is written, a cancel ending its turn as a capture recorded it, 1v). A view-model sees the interfaces only, so a later port swaps a real CLI in under them.

**May import:** this layer, Data and Infrastructure, and the packages `package.json` lists as dependencies. No React, no `.tsx` file, no Node built-in, no module above.

**Enforced by:** [`tests/architecture/layering`](../../tests/architecture/layering/LayeringCheck.ts), which reads [`modules.json`](../../modules.json) and fails an upward or undeclared edge, React below Presentation, anything from outside the prototype, and this card when its **Modules** line disagrees with the map.

**Changing this layer:** a new module is a folder `source/business/<module>/`, its entry in `modules.json` with what it may depend on, its tests at `tests/business/<module>/`, and its name on this card's **Modules** line, in backticks. Its name and its interfaces' names are the Mac app's ([its card](../../../../src/darwin/source/business/CLAUDE.md)).
