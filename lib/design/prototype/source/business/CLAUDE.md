# The Business layer

**Position.** Third of the four ([the design's ADR 0001](../../../../documentation/adrs/design/react-design-prototype.md), applying [GUI ADR 0001](../../../../documentation/adrs/gui/layer-enforcement.md)): the services the view-models consume, behind the Mac app's own interfaces, so a later port swaps a real CLI in under the same names.

**Modules:** `reads` (CommandRunning, its five read outcomes and four command outcomes, and ReadRunner over the process stand-in), `jobs` (a job as a stream of updates, ended last, replayed from a recording on the clock; every child the app runs in one list, and a job's progress folded from its updates, 1h), `session` (a chat from ChatStarting, replaying a recorded machine-mode stream, a turn a send, waiting at each question), `media` (a clip's record read from its video.generation document, the media library over the folders the CLI writes into, the generate form as its argument list, the engine a video model runs on, and the install a refusal names, 1k; a composition's project read from its video.project document, an edit's diff, the compose form as its argument list, the store's voice models and whether ffmpeg burns captions, 1l; a speech's record read from its voice.speech document, the folders' speeches and the say form as its argument list, 1m). The first three are design 1d's; the ADR lists all four.

**What they hold**, each under the Mac app's name: `CommandRunning`, `ReadOutcome`, `CommandOutcome`, `ReadWithStderr`, `ReadRunner` and `SystemReading` (the `system` document, 1e), and the reads a session's header and chooser take (1u): `Field`, `ModelKind`, `ModelRow` and `ModelInfo` (`models list` and `models info`), `SuiteFootprint` and `Suites` (`models status --suite` and `config get suites`), and `Symphony` (`symphonies list`; read whole by the Library, 1n), a resumed chat's turns (1v): `SavedChat` (`chats show`); and the saved chats (1g): `ChatSummary`, `ChatDetails` and `RecallListing` (`chats list`, `chats info`, `chats recall`); the store (1h): `ModelRow`'s every field, `MLXKind`, `ModelInfo.read`, `RoleAssignment`, `BackendAdapter` and `AdapterCheck`; Settings' (1i): `CheckReport` and `CheckRow` (`check`), `Permissions`, `AllowedHosts` and `ServerTools` (the config verbs' reads), `Provider`, `ProviderRegistration` and `KeyedProviders` (`providers scan`, `providers register`, `auth list`), `MCPPreset` (`mcp presets`), and `AgentRegistration`, `XcodeCheck` and `MCPServerEntry` (Xcode's); the Library's and Review's (1n): `AgentEntry` and `MCPServer` (`agents list`, `mcp list`, in `Library.ts`), `Symphony` whole -- its source, file, input and stages -- and `SymphonyPlay` (`symphonies list | show | play`), and `ReviewFinding`, `ReviewRun`, `ReviewTarget`, `ReviewSweep` and `ReviewSkill` (`Review.ts`: `review mr`, `review targets`, `review sweep`, `review skills list | show`); Training's (1j): `TrainingRun`, `VersionLedger`, `TrainingStatus`, `PipelineRunRow`, `PipelineRun`, `PipelineSpec`, `CycleHeadline`, `CycleHistory`, `TrainingRegime`, `TrainingKit` and `TrainingDataset` (`train status`, `train versions`, `train pipeline list | status`, `train cycle status`, `train regime list`, `datasets kits | list`); `JobStarting`, `JobRunning`, `JobUpdate`, `JobEnd`, `JobStarter` and `SupervisedJob` (its end held back by its rule's `exitAfter`, 1v; listing each job in the registry it is given, 1h), `ChildRegistry` (each child's arguments beside its label, and one child's Stop by its id, 1k) and `JobProgress` (1h; a pipeline's stages with their runs and scores, the trainer's iteration and a cycle pass's narration, 1j), a job's stderr played as its busy line where its rule says `busy` (1j); `ChatStarting`, `ChatSession`, `SessionEvent`, `SessionEnd`, `ChatLaunch`, `ApogeeClient` and `MachineSession` (a freeze at a named event, an exit replayed after the input closes, a restart, a continuation picked by what is written, a cancel ending its turn as a capture recorded it, 1v); and the media (1k): `ClipRecord` and `MediaLibrary` (`MediaRecords.ts`: the `video.generation` record, the scan of the folders, the chain, a free name, the install a refusal names), `ClipForm` (the generate form as `video generate`'s arguments, in the order the CLI's verdicts recorded taking), `MediaEngine` and `MediaModel` (the engine a store's video model runs on) and `InstallConsent` (the install a refusal names, asked before it runs), with `RuntimeInstall` in `jobs` (an install as its card draws it) and `JobProgress`' media parts (the runtime loaded, the step, the frames and their assembly), as the Mac keeps them; and the compositions (1l): `ProjectRecord`, `SceneChange` and `ProjectLibrary` (`Projects.ts`: the `video.project` document, the folders' projects, the project beside a video, an edit's diff and whether a render's changes are its preview's), `ComposeForm` (`video compose`'s arguments, `--planner` and an edit's settings besides the Mac's), `CaptionsAbility` (`check`'s `ffmpeg captions` row), `MediaModel.voiceModels`, a free name kept clear of the `.srt` beside it, and `JobProgress`' scene and segment; a job's `writes` played into the stand-in's folders by `SupervisedJob` (1l); and the speeches (1m): `SpeechRecord` and `MediaLibrary.speeches` (`MediaRecords.ts`: the `voice.speech` document, its sentences laid onto the file's own offsets, the sentence at a time, a record without its segments passed over and said) and `SayForm` (`voice say`'s arguments, in the order the CLI's verdicts recorded taking); and Knowledge's and the Code graph's (1o): `KnowledgeRecord`, `KnowledgeQuery`, `CollectionRow`, `CollectionInfo` (with the Vectors line in `embed info`'s own words) and `RawConversation` (`Knowledge.ts`: `knowledge list | query | info --raw`, `embed list | info`), and `GraphNode`, `GraphEdge`, `GraphQuery`, `GraphExplain`, `GraphStats`, `GraphCommunity`, `GraphDecision` and `GraphReport` (`Graph.ts`: `graph query | explain | stats | report`). A view-model sees the interfaces only, so a later port swaps a real CLI in under them.

**May import:** this layer, Data and Infrastructure, and the packages `package.json` lists as dependencies. No React, no `.tsx` file, no Node built-in, no module above.

**Enforced by:** [`tests/architecture/layering`](../../tests/architecture/layering/LayeringCheck.ts), which reads [`modules.json`](../../modules.json) and fails an upward or undeclared edge, React below Presentation, anything from outside the prototype, and this card when its **Modules** line disagrees with the map.

**Changing this layer:** a new module is a folder `source/business/<module>/`, its entry in `modules.json` with what it may depend on, its tests at `tests/business/<module>/`, and its name on this card's **Modules** line, in backticks. Its name and its interfaces' names are the Mac app's ([its card](../../../../src/darwin/source/business/CLAUDE.md)).
