Group commit. A transaction is durable once its log record reaches disk. A single fsync() makes every record appended before it durable, so committers that arrive inside the same flush window share one write instead of paying for their own. Under load the window fills on its own: while one flush is in flight, the next commits queue behind it and go out together.
